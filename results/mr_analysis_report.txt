--- Methods ---
MR Methods (pure R implementation, no MendelianRandomization package):
  1. Inverse-Variance Weighted (IVW) - primary analysis
  2. MR-Egger regression - pleiotropy assessment
  3. Weighted median - robust to outliers
  4. Weighted mode-based estimation (Hartwig 2017)
All implemented in pure R due to R 4.6.0 package incompatibility.

--- Data Limitations ---
1. OGT cis-eQTL data from eQTLGen (blood) is sparse on chrX.
   GTEx v8 Heart LV tissue-specific eQTLs recommended for definitive analysis.
2. Forward MR (OGT → HF) uses literature-based GTEx Heart LV cis-eQTL instruments.
3. Bidirectional MR (HF → OGT) uses HF GWAS instruments.
4. Colocalization requires shared SNPs between eQTL and GWAS datasets.
